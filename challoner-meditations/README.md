# Challoner's Meditations for Every Day in the Year

Bishop Richard Challoner's *Considerations upon Christian Truths and Christian Duties, Digested into Meditations for Every Day in the Year* (1753), corrected from three scans of two nineteenth-century printings.

## Source

- **Edition:** Philadelphia: Cummiskey, 1874 (both parts), and Dublin: James Duffy, 1859. The Webb edition on archive.org (MN5108ucmf_1) is Fr. John Bell's abridgement and was not used.
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/ConsiderationsUponChristianTruths
  - https://archive.org/details/Meditations1859
  - https://archive.org/details/considerationsup00chaluoft

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `meditations.json` | `fixed`: the meditations for each date, by `MM-DD`; `movable`: those of the movable feasts and seasons, by days from Easter (`+0` is Easter Sunday). Each has a `title` and `paragraphs` |
| `meditations.txt` | The same, as plain text |

## Method

The OCR of each scan was compared word by word, and where the scans disagreed the reading of the majority (or, for two scans, the one that is a dictionary word) was kept. Running heads, page numbers and broken lines were then removed, and common OCR confusions of the typeface repaired.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 0.57% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.
- The third scan covers only July to December, so the first half of the year was voted between two scans only.

About 300,549 words.
