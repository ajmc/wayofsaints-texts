# Lives of the Saints, with Reflections for Every Day in the Year (1894)

The short lives and daily reflections compiled from Alban Butler's *Lives of the Saints*, in the one-volume edition published by Benziger Brothers, corrected from three scans of the printed book.

## Source

- **Edition:** *Lives of the Saints, with Reflections for Every Day in the Year*, compiled from the "Lives of the Saints" by Alban Butler (New York, Cincinnati and Chicago: Benziger Brothers, 1894).
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/livesofsaintswith00butl
  - https://archive.org/details/livesofsaintswit00butl
  - https://archive.org/details/LivesOfTheSaints1894

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `lives.json` | 400 lives: `month`, `day`, `title`, `life` (a list of paragraphs) and `reflection` |
| `lives.txt` | The same, as plain text |

## Method

The OCR of each scan was compared word by word, and where the scans disagreed the reading of the majority (or, for two scans, the one that is a dictionary word) was kept. Running heads, page numbers and broken lines were then removed, and common OCR confusions of the typeface repaired. The decorated first letter of each life, which OCR drops, was restored, and a few lives were corrected by hand.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 0.07% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.
- The days are those of the book, which follows the calendar of its time, not today's.

About 132,060 words.
