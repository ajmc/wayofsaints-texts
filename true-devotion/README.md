# True Devotion to Mary (St. Louis de Montfort)

St. Louis de Montfort, *True Devotion to Mary* (c. 1712), in the English translation below, corrected from the scans of the printed book.

## Source

- **Edition:** Translated by Frederick William Faber (Burns & Lambert, 1863).
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/TreatiseTrueDevotionBlessedVirgin
  - https://archive.org/details/ATreatiseOnTheTrueDevotion

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `book.json` | `title`, `author`, `written`, `edition` and `chapters`, each with `part`, `title`, `subtitle` and `paragraphs` |
| `book.txt` | The same, as plain text |

## Method

The OCR of each scan was compared word by word, and where the scans disagreed the reading of the majority (or, for two scans, the one that is a dictionary word) was kept. Running heads, page numbers and broken lines were then removed, and common OCR confusions of the typeface repaired.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 2.71% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.
- The Latin quotations are kept as printed.

About 44,512 words.
