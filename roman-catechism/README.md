# The Roman Catechism (The Council of Trent)

The Council of Trent, *The Roman Catechism* (1566), in the English translation below, corrected from the scans of the printed book.

## Source

- **Edition:** The Catechism of the Council of Trent for Parish Priests, translated by John A. McHugh, O.P., and Charles J. Callan, O.P. (Joseph F. Wagner, 1923).
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/catechismofcounc0000jose
  - https://archive.org/details/catechismofcounc0000pope
  - https://archive.org/details/catechismofcounc0000cath
  - https://archive.org/details/bwb_P9-DTM-981

## Method

The OCR of each scan was compared word by word, and where the scans disagreed the reading of the majority (or, for two scans, the one that is a dictionary word) was kept. Running heads, page numbers and broken lines were then removed, and common OCR confusions of the typeface repaired.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 0.16% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.

About 186,598 words.
