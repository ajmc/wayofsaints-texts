# The Dark Night of the Soul (St. John of the Cross)

St. John of the Cross, *The Dark Night of the Soul* (c. 1585), in the English translation below, corrected from the scans of the printed book.

## Source

- **Edition:** Translated by David Lewis, revised by Benedict Zimmerman, O.C.D. (Thomas Baker, 1916; 1924 printing).
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/darknightofsoul01john
  - https://archive.org/details/bwb_Y0-BYN-878

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `book.json` | `title`, `author`, `written`, `edition` and `chapters`, each with `part`, `title`, `subtitle` and `paragraphs` |
| `book.txt` | The same, as plain text |

## Method

The OCR of each scan was compared word by word, and where the scans disagreed the reading of the majority (or, for two scans, the one that is a dictionary word) was kept. Running heads, page numbers and broken lines were then removed, and common OCR confusions of the typeface repaired.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 2.33% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.
- The Spanish of the stanzas is kept as printed.

About 47,385 words.
