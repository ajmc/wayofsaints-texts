# The Way of Perfection (St. Teresa of Ávila)

St. Teresa of Ávila, *The Way of Perfection* (1566), in the English translation below, corrected from the scans of the printed book.

## Source

- **Edition:** Translated by the Benedictines of Stanbrook (Thomas Baker, 1911).
- **Scans** on the Internet Archive:
  - https://archive.org/details/wayofperfection00tere_0

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `book.json` | `title`, `author`, `written`, `edition` and `chapters`, each with `part`, `title`, `subtitle` and `paragraphs` |
| `book.txt` | The same, as plain text |

## Method

There is one scan, so the text is its OCR cleaned, not voted between scans.

## Known limits

- **OCR errors remain.** About 0.64% of lower-case words are not in a spelling dictionary. That figure is an upper bound: it also counts British spellings, Latin and proper names. The text has been corrected by machine, not proofread by a person; corrections are welcome.

About 71,120 words.
